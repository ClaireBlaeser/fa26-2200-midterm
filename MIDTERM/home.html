BLOW THINGS UP!

The ATLAS BTU Lab is an inclusive community makerspace facilitating projects at the intersection of Art, Design, Engineering and Architecture. It serves multiple functions as a general workspace, and workshop host.

About Us

MAKE IT. TEST IT. BLOW IT UP.

WHO?

Anyone can gain access to BTU! We welcome makers of all backgrounds and interests in an inclusive, experimental, and productive community.

HOW?

New members attend a 20 minute general orientation covering community expectations, safety and equipment.

WHEN?

Find orientation schedules and upcoming events on the BTU Lab Calendar. Some equipment has separate orientation requirements.

WHAT?

Have questions or want to contribute to the BTU Lab? Get in touch!

WHERE?

ATLAS BTU Lab

320 UCB, 1125 18th St, Boulder, CO 80309

BTU Lab is not a free supply store. But there are many ways to barter, including displaying your work in BTU, tagging us in social media, offering workshops, or volunteering to organize supplies.
